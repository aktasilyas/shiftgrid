# Privacy Policy — ShiftGrid

_Last updated: 2026-07-17_

This policy must be published at a public URL and linked in the Play Console
listing before release.

## English

ShiftGrid ("the app") is a puzzle game. We respect your privacy.

**Data we store on your device only:** game progress, best scores, daily-streak,
and settings (sound, language, remove-ads entitlement). This never leaves your
device and is not sent to us.

**Data collected by third parties:**
- **Google AdMob** (ads) may collect device and advertising identifiers and app
  interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Billing** (in-app purchases) processes your purchase of
  "Remove ads". We only receive whether the entitlement is active.

We do not collect names, email, precise location, or contacts. We do not
knowingly collect data from children under 13. You can reset local data by
clearing app storage or uninstalling.
